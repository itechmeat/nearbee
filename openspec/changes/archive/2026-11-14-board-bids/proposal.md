# Proposal: board bids

## Why

Today a Nearbee agent earns only from instant hires and x402 calls at its
fixed price. The market's job board, where a buyer posts a job and agents
bid, is the second way work arrives, and the hackathon video shows it live:
the owner posts a job, a Nearbee agent finds it, bids, the owner awards the
bid in the web app, and the agent does the work through the normal hire
path. D-011 left board bidding out of v1; this change reverses that part
(D-028).

Two defects seen on the live hires of 2026-11-13 are folded in: a
zero-byte output file makes the market refuse the upload with `400
invalid_byte_size`, which declined a whole hire, and the sandbox image
lacks `socat`, which Claude Code's Bash sandbox needs to start.

## What Changes

- A `board` block in `nearbee.yaml` names, per listing handle, what the
  agent bids on: a search query, job kinds, the bid amount (default: the
  listing price), the promised SLA (default: the listing SLA), a cap on
  open bids and an optional allowlist of poster accounts. No block, no
  bidding.
- `nearbee serve` runs a bidder on its own interval (not faster than the
  poll interval): it lists the board per configured listing, filters the
  jobs (query, kind, USDC budget at or above the bid amount, the owner's
  verification level, poster allowlist, not already bid on, slots free),
  runs the listing's intake checks on the job's title and description as
  the brief, and places one bid per job with a proposal generated by code
  from the listing (no model call, no buyer text). Every bid goes through
  a durable bid journal under `<state_dir>/bids/`; a restart never bids
  twice on one job.
- An awarded bid arrives as an ordinary assignment and is served by the
  existing hire path; the bidder reads the award back and logs it.
- `nearbee bids [--json]` lists the bid journal; serve logs every bid
  decision (matched, skipped with the reason, bid sent, awarded).
- Serve skips a zero-byte output file, journals the skip and delivers the
  rest; the sandbox image installs `socat`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `board-bidding`: finding board jobs, choosing them, placing idempotent
  journaled bids, reading their outcome, listing them.

### Modified Capabilities

- `nearbee-config`: the `board` block.
- `market-client`: the board, bid and my-bids calls.
- `hire-serving`: awarded bids served as hires; zero-byte output files.
- `apb-sandbox`: `socat` in the image.

## Stage, plan items, seams

Not one of the six stages of `docs/stages.md`: a post-S6 change for the
demo, like the 2026-11-13 changes. Crates: `nearbee-core` (the bid
journal, the choice of jobs, the proposal, the bidder pass, the zero-byte
skip), `nearbee-market` (the wire types and calls), `nearbee` (config
wiring in serve, `nearbee bids`). The seam rule holds: the board calls
are a second small trait, `Board`, beside `Market` in `nearbee-core`, and
only `nearbee-market` implements it against the market.

## Out of scope

Bid threads (reading and answering the poster before the award), bid
withdrawal and revision, quote requests (`invitedAgentId`), competitions
(entries, not bids), USD jobs (they need Stripe on the owner's account),
price discovery (the amount is configured), and a model-written proposal.

`crates/nearbee` (`lib.rs`: serve and `bids`), `sandbox/Dockerfile`,
